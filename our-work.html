Core Focus Areas

Education

Regular health, women & child health, eye, dental and mental health awareness camps bring basic healthcare and early screening to communities that would otherwise go without it.

Working with local donors and self-help groups, we run mid-day meal and food-security initiatives that address hunger and support healthy growth in children.
