Our Story

Educating Hearts, Empowering Futures.

Mahesh Vidyasagar Memorial School Foundation is a Section 8 not-for-profit organisation committed to education, health, and human dignity. Our work focuses on creating strong foundations for lasting impact among underserved communities.

We work towards school sustainability, healthcare access, nutrition, skill development, and community welfare while building partnerships and opportunities for meaningful social impact.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower underserved communities through education, health, nutrition, skill development, access, dignity, and opportunity.

  • Strengthen school sustainability and learning
  • Deliver health awareness and community care
  • Provide mid-day meals and food security
  • Build employable skills through training

Our Vision

A society where every child and family, regardless of background, has access to quality education, healthcare and nutrition, and the opportunity to live with dignity.

  • Strong foundations for every child
  • Healthy, nourished and confident communities
  • Lasting impact through partnerships

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every decision we make and every community we serve.

Dignity

Every person deserves respect, care and the chance to live with self-respect.

Education First

Learning is the strongest foundation for a child's future and a community's progress.

Compassion

We listen, care and act with empathy for the children and families we serve.

Transparency

We work openly and responsibly, so every supporter can see how their contribution creates impact.

Inclusion

We reach underserved communities so no one is left behind because of background or circumstance.

Sustainability

We build partnerships and programmes designed to create lasting change, not short-term relief.
